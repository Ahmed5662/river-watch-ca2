River Watch Flood Intelligence & Response Centre Irish towns · live decision support

River-led decisions for eight Irish towns.

Operational dashboard for the Duty Officer: live three-day river flow peaks, town-level flood actions, crew deployment and evidence for the Area Manager’s flood-defence study question.

AI can make mistakes. A person makes the final decision. This tool supports tonight’s operational judgement; it does not predict flooding for individual properties.
